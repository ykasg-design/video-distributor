Video Distributor

Video Distributor is a web app that lets creators and brands connect their own TikTok accounts and publish their finished short videos to them.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The owner selects a finished video, writes a caption and chooses the privacy level offered by TikTok.
  3. After the owner confirms, the video is uploaded through the TikTok Content Posting API, either as a draft or as a direct post, and the publish status is shown.
