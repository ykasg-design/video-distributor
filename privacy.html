Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What we collect

When you authorize Video Distributor with TikTok we receive: your TikTok open id, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and access tokens that let us upload or post videos you choose (scopes video.upload and video.publish).

How we use it

Only to show which account is connected and to upload or publish the videos you select, with the caption and privacy settings you choose. We do not read your followers, messages or other videos, and we do not sell or share your data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored on our own computer, are not shared with third parties, and are deleted when you disconnect or when they are no longer needed. Videos are sent directly to TikTok.

Your choices

You can revoke access anytime in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). After revocation we can no longer act on your account and we delete the stored tokens.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: our support page.
